Your money stays yours.

No accounts with us, no servers, no ads. Your data lives on your devices, and in your own Google Drive only if you choose.

    Everything is stored on your device. Nobody else sees your numbers, not even the app maker.

    PC, phone and tablet stay in step through a hidden folder in your Google Drive, with a daily copy kept for the last seven days.

    Works offline

    The whole app lives on your device, so it opens instantly, anywhere.
