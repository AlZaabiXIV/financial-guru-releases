Installing on Windows

  1. Open the file you downloaded, FinancialGuru-Setup.
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer, then open the app from the Start menu.
Why the warning? The app is new and does not yet carry a paid signing certificate. It is safe, and it comes straight from the official releases page.

Installing on Android

  1. Download the file on your phone or tablet, then open it.
  2. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. Tap Install, then open The Financial Guru. If your profiles are on your PC, choose “Get my profiles from Google Drive”.
Samsung phone? If you see “Unknown app blocked”, turn off Auto Blocker for a moment in Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker, install, then turn it back on.
