# The Financial Guru · المرشد المالي

Your personal financial guru, made in the UAE 🇦🇪. Budgets, bills, income, goals and a clear view of the year ahead, on Windows and Android.

🌐 **Website:** https://alzaabixiv.github.io/financial-guru/

➡️ **[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

| Device | File to download |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10 and later** | `FinancialGuru-Setup-<version>.exe`: download it and run it. |
| **Android phones and tablets** | `FinancialGuru-<version>.apk`: download it on your phone and open it. |

Once installed, the app updates itself: when a new version is ready, a pop-up shows what's new. Tap **Update**.

## What's inside

- **Budget:** bills and pay later, income and deductions, everyday spending, savings pots and Shop & Win.
- **Timeline:** a month-by-month view of the next 12 months, so you see tight months coming.
- **Goals and wishlist**, property and car payments, a currency converter and practice markets.
- **A built-in assistant ✨** that answers questions about your money and logs expenses for you.
- **Your data stays yours:** saved on your device, with optional backup to your own Google Drive.
- **English and Arabic**, on PC, phone and tablet.
