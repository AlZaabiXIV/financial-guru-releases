## What it will do

- **Budget:** bills and pay later, income and deductions, everyday spending and savings pots.
- **Timeline:** a month-by-month view of the next 12 months, so you see tight months coming.
- **Goals and wishlist**, property and car payments, and a currency converter.
- **Health** (in development): medicines and reminders, appointments, family health, and your smartwatch data in one place.
- **A built-in assistant ✨** that answers questions about your money and logs expenses for you.
- **Your data stays yours:** saved on your device, with optional backup to your own Google Drive.
- **English and Arabic**, on PC, phone and tablet.
